How the Prophetic People Are Formed

Every student of the School is shaped across three dimensions — spirit, understanding, and dominion.

I

Hearing God

Cultivating intimacy with the Spirit, prophetic sensitivity, worship, and the discipline of waiting on the Lord — the root of all true prophetic ministry.

II

Discerning the Times

Like the sons of Issachar, understanding the seasons — from the restoration of the Tabernacle of David to the rise of new economies of wealth.

III

Building the Kingdom

Turning revelation into action: leadership, stewardship, enterprise, and service that bring Heaven’s order into families, cities, and nations.
