What is TAKO?

TAKO is an Ethereum ERC-20 community meme token. Its official website presents it as a tribute to the origin story of the Shiba Inu, centred on a dog named Tako.

Why call TAKO “the Kingdom’s Gold”?

“Gold” is the Kingdom of David’s metaphor for the intended wealth pathway supporting its vision. It does not mean TAKO is backed by physical gold, redeemable for gold, or guaranteed to increase in value.

What are the supply and token taxes?

The official website reports a total supply of 420,690,000,000 TAKO, 100% circulating, with 0% buy tax and 0% sell tax. Ethereum gas fees and exchange-related costs may still apply. Verify current contract behaviour yourself.

Where can I buy and verify TAKO?

The official website links to Uniswap on Ethereum. Verify this full address on Etherscan before trading: 0x0ee375090f423934ebfbb241a3eb2ee4e01eaf0a.

Is profit or financial security guaranteed?

No. Cryptocurrency prices can be highly volatile, and you could lose your entire investment. Renounced ownership, zero token tax, and community narratives do not eliminate market, liquidity, contract, or wallet risks.
